FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about setting up and running QuberBill.

Do I need to buy expensive POS hardware or billing machines?
No. QuberBill runs directly in the browser on any existing Android phone, tablet, iPad, laptop, or desktop computer. You can connect standard USB or Bluetooth barcode guns and thermal printers with zero special hardware required.
How do Dynamic UPI QR code payments work?
When you finish an invoice, QuberBill generates an exact amount-coded QR code on your screen or thermal receipt. The customer scans it with GPay, PhonePe, or Paytm and the exact bill amount is pre-filled. Payments go straight to your bank account with zero swipe fees.
Can I print receipts on 2-inch and 3-inch thermal printers?
Yes! QuberBill supports direct ESC/POS thermal printing for both 58mm (2-inch) and 80mm (3-inch) USB and Bluetooth printers. You can also print full A4/A5 tax invoices on regular printers or share paperless bills via WhatsApp.
Is my business and sales data secure?
Yes, completely. Your sales transactions, customer Khata, inventory, and profit data are stored in your private database with automatic daily cloud backups and SSL encryption. Only authorized staff and owners can access the system.
